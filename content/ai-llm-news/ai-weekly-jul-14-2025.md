This week’s AI landscape spans major government bets, tech disruption, and regulatory evolution, marking a critical moment for AI’s future.

## 1. xAI & OpenAI Land $200 M DoD Contracts

{{< blog-image src="/images/ai-weekly-july-14-2025_01.png" alt="DoD AI contracts" >}}

Elon Musk’s **xAI** and **OpenAI** have each been awarded up to $200 million contracts by the U.S. Department of Defense to develop agentic AI tools across military, intelligence, and federal use cases. xAI’s “Grok for Government” suite, featuring its Grok 4 model, joins similar deals with Google and Anthropic  
([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/us-department-defense-awards-contracts-google-xai-2025-07-14/)).

## 2. OpenAI Close to Launching AI-Powered Web Browser

{{< blog-image src="/images/ai-weekly-july-14-2025_02.png" alt="OpenAI AI browser" >}}

OpenAI is reportedly finalizing an **AI-augmented browser**, embedding ChatGPT and its Operator agent into the browsing experience. Built on Chromium and anticipated to launch imminently, the browser aims to challenge Google Chrome by offering in-context AI help, real-time summarization, and seamless agent integrations  
([theguardian.com](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/jul/14/us-military-xai-deal-elon-musk)).

## 3. OpenAI Delays Release of Open-Weight Model Over Safety Concerns

{{< blog-image src="/images/ai-weekly-july-14-2025_03.png" alt="OpenAI open-weight delay" >}}

OpenAI has indefinitely delayed its open-weight model release, citing the need for additional safety checks. CEO Sam Altman emphasized that once model weights are public, they cannot be retracted—highlighting a caution-first approach amid growing concerns around model misuse  
([timesofindia.indiatimes.com](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/openai-to-launch-ai-powered-web-browser-heres-how-it-may-hurtgooglechrome/articleshow/122387677.cms)).
